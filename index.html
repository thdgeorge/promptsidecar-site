PromptSidecar

PromptSidecar is a lightweight Google Docs add-on for finding, copying, navigating, and formatting reusable AI prompt blocks while keeping the Google Doc as the source of truth.

This page provides the privacy policy, terms of service, support information, and draft tester opt-out instructions for the current test version.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

What PromptSidecar accesses

PromptSidecar works with the Google Doc that you currently have open and only after you authorize the add-on. It reads marked prompt sections so it can list them in the sidebar, copy their text, jump to their location, and apply formatting when you explicitly request it.

Data storage and sharing

PromptSidecar has no external application server, prompt database, analytics service, or telemetry system. The developer does not receive or store your document content or prompt text. PromptSidecar does not sell personal data or document content.

Copying a prompt places the selected text on your local clipboard through the add-on interface. Google may process information as part of Google Docs, Google Apps Script, OAuth, and Google Workspace Marketplace under Google's own terms and privacy policies.

Retention

The developer does not retain copies of your Google Docs content or prompts because PromptSidecar does not transmit them to a developer-operated backend.

Permissions

The add-on is designed to request only the Google permissions needed to work with the current document and display its Google Docs interface. Platform-provided identity scopes may also appear as part of Google's Marketplace/OAuth flow.

Changes

If PromptSidecar's data practices materially change, this policy will be updated before those changes are introduced to testers.
